# MediPortal Quick Start Guide

## 🩺 For Doctors: Clinical Excellence
1. **Patient Chart**: View longitudinal vitals, lab trends, and RPM data in a single dashboard.
2. **AI Summary**: Use the "Generate AI Summary" button to condense complex histories into actionable insights.
3. **Telemedicine**: Click "Start Consultation" from the schedule to launch a secure video call.
4. **Clinical Notes**: Draft notes in real-time. Use the AI Assistant to verify ICD-10 codes.

## 🩺 For Nurses: Care Coordination
1. **Registration**: Quick-enroll patients using the "Patients" tab.
2. **Vitals**: Use the Mobile App for bedside rounding and instant vitals sync.
3. **Triage**: Utilize the AI Symptom Checker to prioritize emergency cases.

## 💊 For Pharmacists: Inventory Control
1. **Dispensing**: Scan barcodes to verify prescriptions against batch expiry dates (FEFO).
2. **Stock Alerts**: Monitor the dashboard for "Low Stock" notifications.
3. **Audit**: Periodic inventory audits are logged for regulatory compliance.

## 💳 For Billing Admins: Financial Integrity
1. **Invoice**: Bills are auto-generated from clinical lab orders and consultations.
2. **Taxation**: Local taxes (VAT/GST) are applied automatically based on your branch settings.
3. **Claims**: Submit insurance claims directly with attached clinical justification.

## 🛡️ Data Privacy & AI Safety
- All patient data is encrypted. 
- The AI Assistant is prohibited from storing patient names; it only processes anonymized clinical context.
